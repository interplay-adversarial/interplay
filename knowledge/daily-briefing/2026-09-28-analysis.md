### 1. RSI Taxonomy for Recursive Self-Improving AI Safety

**Paper**: [arXiv:2609.31186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31186)  
**Title**: "A new taxonomy for evaluating safety of recursive self-improving AI"

#### Summary
First systematic taxonomy for RSI (Recursive Self-Improving) AI systems with 320 distinct configurations across 4 axes:
- **Architecture type**: fixed vs mutable
- **Improvement target**: code, weights, data, architecture, prompt
- **Safety mechanism**: oversight, alignment, robustness
- **Evolutionary scenario**: supervised, unsupervised, cooperative, adversarial

#### Relevance to interplay-adversarial
**Direct alignment**: The taxonomy formalizes "unsupervised, fixed-architecture RSI over behavioral policy" - exactly what happens when agents optimize goal completion over long horizons and find policy-violating shortcuts.

**Gap addressed**: Current library lacks:
- Framework for evaluating self-improving agents
- Risk-discovery protocols for different RSI quadrants
- Systematic categorization of agent security failures

### 2. Belief Self-Distillation (BSD)

**Paper**: [arXiv:2609.31603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31603)  
**Title**: "Belief Self-Distillation: A Unified Read-Write Framework for LLM Beliefs About Users"

#### Summary
Bridges linear probing (accurate but shallow) and causal intervention (causal but coarse) by distilling linear belief probes into directional read vectors. Achieves 18.3 percentage point improvement over baselines for belief extraction.

#### Relevance to interplay-adversarial
**Potential application**: Could detect when models form biased beliefs about users (demographic attributes) that lead to differential treatment - directly relevant to cultural bias evaluation.

**Gap addressed**: Current probes test for output bias but don't measure the internal belief formation that drives it.

### 3. DIAL - Position-Debiasing for LLM-as-a-Judge

**Paper**: [arXiv:2609.31215](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31215)  
**Title**: "DIAL: Unified Position-Debiasing and Human-Preference Calibration for LLM-as-a-Judge"

#### Summary
Addresses position bias (60% agreement with human judgments) and human divergence in LLM judges. Compresses gap between LLM-judge and human-judge rankings by 34-48% across MT-Bench, ChatArena, AlpacaEval.

#### Relevance to interplay-adversarial
**Direct relevance**: Interplay uses LLM judges for probe quality scoring (probe_quality_score column). DIAL's debiasing could improve judge calibration.

**Gap addressed**: Current judge may have position bias and human-divergent preferences affecting scoring reliability.
